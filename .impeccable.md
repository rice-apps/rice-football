## Design Context

### Users
Sprint developers testing generic CSV/JSON parsing and mock APIs before stakeholder requirements are confirmed.

### Brand Personality
Clear, practical, restrained. Help developers understand their data and errors.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the existing dev prototypes and shared Rice blue/gray tokens. Product design remains provisional.

### Design Principles
- Keep prototypes under `/dev/`.
- Reuse existing styles and tokens.
- Show readable errors and bounded previews.
- Avoid vendor-specific assumptions and speculative product features.
